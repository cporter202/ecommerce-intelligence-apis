# Price monitoring workflow

1. Choose sources and permitted geographic marketplaces.
2. Collect product identifiers, URLs, prices, currency, stock, seller, and retrieval time.
3. Resolve products by stable IDs such as ASIN, SKU, UPC, EAN, or a canonical product URL.
4. Store historical observations instead of overwriting the previous price.
5. Alert only on meaningful changes after normalizing currency, promotions, and stock status.

Current price data and historical price intelligence are different products. Decide which one you need before estimating cost.
